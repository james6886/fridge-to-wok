# Fridge to Wok 冰箱到炒鍋

A recipe finder with 210 dishes, mostly Taiwanese and Chinese, plus other Asian and Western food.

- Pick what's in your fridge and see what you can cook now, or what's missing 1-2 items
- Menu filters: healthy, low calorie, dessert, kids meal, vegetarian, vegan, breakfast, spicy and more
- Step-by-step instructions with a time for every step, plus a cook mode with timers
- English and Traditional Chinese (繁體中文)
- Photos from Wikimedia Commons, credited on each recipe

Static site: open index.html, or deploy the folder as-is (no build step).
